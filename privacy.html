Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 August 2026

What Ceremli stores

Ceremli stores account details and the wedding-planning information you choose to enter, including wedding details, guest names and contact details, RSVPs, dietary information, event attendance, seating, budget information, invitations and supplier enquiries.

Why we use the information

We use this information to provide the Ceremli wedding-planning service, authenticate your account, deliver invitations and password-reset emails when requested, process plan payments, operate RSVP tools, and keep your planning information available to you.

Service providers

Ceremli may use infrastructure and service providers such as Railway for hosting, Resend for transactional email and Stripe for payment processing. These providers receive only the information needed to perform the relevant service.

Your guests

If you add guest information, you are responsible for having an appropriate reason to use that information for your wedding planning and invitations. Avoid entering information you do not need.

Data access and deletion

From Account settings you can download a copy of your Ceremli planning data and permanently delete your account. Deleting an account removes the account and associated wedding-planning records from Ceremli's application database.

Security

Ceremli uses password hashing, authenticated sessions, CSRF protection on account actions, rate limits on sensitive endpoints and HTTPS in production. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

Ceremli uses an essential login-session cookie so signed-in features work. It is not used for advertising.

Contact

Before public commercial launch, Ceremli's operator contact details should be added here for privacy requests.
